    Why this shape

    Facets on the left, results on the right — same bones as Christian's internal tools, but the chrome is stripped down to something that can drop into a Wix Studio content block rather than run as its own app.

    Cards, not a table

    Each card is a range (Atlantic, Pacific Vertical, Silverstone…), not a single SKU — matching how nardicompressori.com actually pages things. "View range" links straight to the mapped URL.

    Real photos, not placeholders

    Card art is pulled live from nardicompressori.com's own image CDN (square-cropped from the site's 3000×3000 source files) and embedded directly, so this stays self-contained and works offline once sent to you.

    Brochure & catalog links

    Each card links out to the matching document(s) from the Link Management sheet — same SharePoint links nardicompressori.com already uses on its own pages. Where nothing's on file, the card says so honestly rather than guessing.

    Paintball/PCP is its own Use category

    Shooting 100 was initially folded into Breathing Air — corrected: it's its own standalone category, not a subset of Breathing Air, same treatment as CNG got. Now its own top-level Use pill, linking to its real `/shooting-100-compressor` page.

    Format facet is conditional

    Portable/Vertical only makes sense for Atlantic & Pacific, so it's visually present but reads as secondary until a Use filter narrows into that territory — never hidden outright, per how you described the field.

    Booster intake, now real

    CM, CB, BM and BON2 now show a real intake range (red chip) pulled from the workbook's MP Pumps / BON Pumps tabs — the Products tab itself has no intake value for boosters (they take pre-compressed air in, not atmosphere), so this had to come from the same kind of secondary lookup table as Oilfree FAD.

    CNG is its own Use category

    Confirmed standalone, not a subset of Industrial — it's exclusive to compressed natural gas, a distinct fuel-specific line. ATEX is an optional certification some CNG units carry, not a separate line (that's why some spreadsheet CNG rows show Catalog = ATEX). Now its own top-level Use pill, sixth alongside Breathing Air / Industrial / Medical & Dental / Oil-Free.

    Scope, deliberately narrow

    Christian's Industry tool does real sizing math — multi-unit arrays, Master/Slave staging, gas-density derating. Confirmed with Tom that's out of scope here: this tool identifies a single matching machine only, and says so explicitly (banner above), pointing anyone with a more complex need to a specialist instead of guessing.

    Sliders now have real low ends

    Max Pressure, Motor Size, and Air Delivery are now proper dual-handle min/max ranges (Pressure's low handle existed visually last time but wasn't actually wired to anything — fixed). Drag either end; they can't cross past each other.

    Air Intake, kept even though it's not always meaningful

    Real ambient-draw values from the Products tab for Oilfree/Medical/Hookah lines. For Atlantic, Pacific, Nitrox, Shooting, Nardi ONE, and the boosters, the spreadsheet has no intake spec at all (it's "/" — these publish delivery/charging rate instead) — those lines always match regardless of where this slider sits, so the field can't accidentally hide them, per Tom's call to keep it in even where it isn't a real differentiator.
